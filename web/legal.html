Privacy, Terms & Licenses

Short version: your files are converted inside your browser and never sent anywhere.

Privacy

  • Your files stay on your device. Every conversion and merge runs in your browser. Files are never uploaded, stored or seen by us.
  • No accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Hosting. The site is served by Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel may record technical request data (such as IP address, browser and time) for security and operations.
  • Audio & video engine. The first audio or video conversion downloads the FFmpeg engine from the jsDelivr CDN. That request reveals your IP address to jsDelivr, as any download would. Your files are not part of it.
